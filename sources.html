Sources & corrections

Code 3 Docket tells real police chases and high-profile cases from the record. This page explains where our material comes from and how to tell us when we got something wrong.

01Where the footage comes from

  • Bodycam, dashcam and police helicopter video that police departments and other agencies released to the public, usually on their own websites or YouTube channels.
  • Press conferences and official statements from those agencies.
  • When released footage leaves gaps, records we request under public records laws. We say on screen when a clip came from a request.

Every clip is labeled on screen with the agency that released it, and each episode description links to the original sources.

02What we add

  • Narration, maps, timelines and diagrams are our own work. Maps and diagrams are labeled as ours, so they are not mistaken for official records.
  • We trim the footage to tell the case. When we show a moment out of order, we label it on screen. Apart from blurring faces and plates and muting names for privacy, we do not alter what the recordings show.

03How we check facts

  • We check what we say against court records, agency statements and established news reports.
  • When sources disagree, we say so, and we attribute claims ("police said", "prosecutors said").
  • We report the status of each case as of the publish date. People who are charged are presumed innocent until proven guilty in court.

04Names and privacy

We leave out the names of bystanders, victims and minors, and we blur the faces and license plates of bystanders when they can be recognized. If you appear in one of our episodes and want your name, face or plate removed, write to us. We review every request and blur or cut what we can.

05Corrections

If something in an episode is wrong or out of date, email code3docket@gmail.com with the video link, the timestamp and what is wrong. If we confirm the error, we correct it in the description and the pinned comment, with the date of the correction. Agencies, courts and rights holders can use the same address.

06Independence

Code 3 Docket is an independent channel. It is not affiliated with any police department, court or government agency.
